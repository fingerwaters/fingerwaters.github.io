Islamic miracle narrations and evidence

Prophet Muhammad Miracle Narrations

Relevant Evidences in Islam: Hadith References

This page presents hadith references concerning water flowing from the hand of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), along with similar narrations. This is one of the most important narratives in Islamic sources. It is placed within the broader context of evidence of prophethood, and the role of hadith literature.

In Islamic scholarship, this topic is studied alongside prophetic miracle narrations, the evidence of the message brought by the Prophet, and the reliability of the hadith collections that preserve these narrations. The purpose of this page is to make those references easier to access for readers who are searching for related terms such as evidence, miracle, Islam, and Prophet Muhammad.

The hadith translations and other information in the links here belong to the websites on which they are published. The information given here about the relevant book/section, chapter (bāb), or the preceding or following hadith, or chains of transmissions was researched to make it easier to locate the mentioned hadith in its source.

The hadith of water flowing from the Prophet Muhammad's hand is not only a historical narration; it is also understood as a major argument about evidence of prophethood in Islam.

Importance of the hadith

The miracle hadiths of water from the Prophet's hand is one of the narrations that are evidence of propethood in Islam.

Evidence of prophethood

Hadith narrations concerning miracles such as water flowing from the hand of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) are considered important evidence of his prophethood.

Hadith sources

Collections such as Sahih Muslim, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sunan ad-Darimi, Jami` at-Tirmidhi, and Muwatta Malik remain essential references for studying this subject.
